Gravity Flip Ultimate

You spawn in a dome lobby: fly into the Practice, VS AI or Ranked portal. Each point starts with the disc launching from the centre after a 10 second countdown. Zero-gravity arena with obstacles. Slap and drag the walls with your hands to move, bank the disc off the angled walls, and hit the glowing green hexagon on the far wall with the disc to score. Guard the cyan hexagon behind you.
